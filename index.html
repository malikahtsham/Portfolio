FAQ

Questions clients ask before we start.

How long does local SEO take to show results?

Most local businesses see Google Maps and search impressions move within 4 to 8 weeks, and calls or leads within 2 to 3 months. Timing depends on competition and how much the website and Google Business Profile need fixing. My Lexington cleaning client started getting leads in 2.5 months and reached 9.88K Google clicks in 3 months.

One-time work starts at $149 for Google Business Profile setup. For comparison, Ahrefs' survey of 439 SEO providers found freelance local SEO averages about $1,150 per month.

What decides who ranks in the Google Map Pack?

Google ranks Map Pack results on three factors: relevance (how well your categories, services and content match the search), distance from the searcher, and prominence (reviews, links, citations and reputation). You can't change distance, so I improve relevance and prominence through your Google Business Profile, service pages, reviews and citations.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews?

AI tools recommend businesses they can clearly identify and trust. That takes the same name, address and phone everywhere, service pages that answer real customer questions in the first sentence, steady reviews, LocalBusiness schema and mentions on trusted local sites. I check where you appear in ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews today, then close the gaps.

No. Nobody controls Google's rankings, and Google itself says no one can guarantee a #1 ranking. What I can promise is work you can see: a fully optimized profile, stronger service pages, steady reviews and citations, and monthly reports from your own Search Console and local rank grid.
